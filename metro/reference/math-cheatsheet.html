Math cheat sheet

Everything from lessons 02–06. Read it once on Tuesday 20.10, not on the morning of the exam.

1 · Matrices lesson

  • Add / subtract only if same size (rows × cols). Otherwise: not defined.
  • Position by position: (A+B)ij = aij + bij
  • kA: multiply every entry by k.
  • X + A = B ⇒ X = B − A
  • Product AB: row of A · column of B. Needs cols(A) = rows(B). AB ≠ BA.

2 · Solving functions lesson

  • Evaluate: substitute with brackets: (−2)² = 4
  • Linear: undo operations, same on both sides. Check by substituting.
  • Factor x² + bx + c: two numbers, product c, sum b.
  • x² = k ⇒ x = ±√k
  • Quadratic formula: x = (−b ± √(b² − 4ac)) / 2a
  • D = b² − 4ac: >0 two roots, =0 one, <0 none.
  • Two lines meet: set equal, solve for x, then y.
  • f(g(x)): inside first.

4 · Mean and median lesson

  • Mean = sum ÷ n
  • Median: sort first; middle value at position (n + 1)/2; even n → mean of the two middle values.
  • Mode = most frequent. Range = max − min.
  • Frequency table: mean = Σ(x·f) ÷ Σf
  • Missing value: total = mean × n, subtract the known ones.
  • Outliers pull the mean, barely move the median.

5 · Probability lesson

  • P(A) = favourable / total (equally likely outcomes)
  • P(not A) = 1 − P(A)
  • Independent AND: P(A)·P(B)
  • Without replacement: P(A)·P(B | A), so the second fraction has one fewer in the total
  • OR: P(A) + P(B) − P(A and B)
  • At least one = 1 − P(none)
  • Two dice: 36 outcomes. Sum 7 is most likely (6/36).

Exam habits

  • Show every step; box the final answer.
  • Circle negative numbers before adding.
  • Plug solutions back in when you have 10 seconds.
  • Fractions are fine; simplify if time allows.

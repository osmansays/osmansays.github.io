Verb Conjugation: Present & Past Tense

🎯 How Swedish Verbs Work

Present Tense: All verbs add -R to the base form

jobba → jobbar, äta → äter, köpa → köper

Past Tense: Group 1 (AR verbs) add -DE, Group 2 (ER verbs) modify the ending

jobbar → jobbade, äter → åt, köper → köpte

Exercise 1: Present Tense Beginner
Fill in the correct present tense form:

Jag _____ (tala) svenska varje dag.

Hint: This is an AR verb
Answer: talar (I speak Swedish every day)
Exercise 2: Past Tense Beginner
Fill in the correct past tense form:

Jag _____ (äta) pizza igår kväll.

Hint: Irregular verb - "ät"
Answer: åt (I ate pizza yesterday evening)
Exercise 3: Mixed Tenses Intermediate
Fill in both present and past forms:

Han _____ (köpa) en bil varje år. Förra året _____ han en Tesla.

Answer: köper / köpte (He buys a car every year. Last year he bought a Tesla)

Word Order: The Golden Rule

🎯 Swedish Word Order Rules

Position 1 (Fundament): Can be 1+ words (PERSON, PLACE, or TIME)

Position 2 (VERB): Always comes 2nd - this is the GOLDEN RULE

Position 3+: Subject (if not in position 1), adverbials (inte, alltid, ofta), object, adverbs

Jag | talar | svenska | varje dag. (I speak Swedish every day)

Varje dag | talar | jag | svenska. (Every day I speak Swedish - same meaning, different emphasis)

Exercise 1: Basic Word Order Beginner
Arrange the words in correct order:

Words: varje | dag | till | jag | jobbet | går

Hint: Start with "Jag" and remember verb is 2nd
Answer: Jag går till jobbet varje dag. (I go to work every day)
Exercise 2: Fronting the Adverbial Intermediate
Rearrange to put the time adverbial first:

Original: Jag tränar på gymmet på morgonen.

Rearrange starting with "På morgonen":

Hint: After adverbial, verb comes next
Answer: På morgonen tränar jag på gymmet. (In the morning I train at the gym - same meaning, different word order for emphasis)
Exercise 3: Negation Placement Intermediate
Add "inte" (not) in the correct position:

Jag gillar att dricka kaffe.

Rewrite with "inte":

Hint: "inte" goes after the verb
Answer: Jag gillar inte att dricka kaffe. (I don't like to drink coffee)

Adjectives: En, Ett, Plural Forms

🎯 Swedish Adjective Rules

EN nouns: Adjective stays as is → en rolig film

ETT nouns: Adjective adds -T → ett roligt jobb

Plural: Adjective adds -A → två roliga filmer

rolig / roligt / roliga, dyr / dyrt / dyra, stor / stort / stora

Exercise 1: Gender Agreement Beginner
Fill in the correct adjective form:

En _____ (stor) hund och ett _____ (stor) hus

Answer: stor / stort (A big dog and a big house)
Exercise 2: Plural Forms Beginner
Rewrite in plural:

Original: En tråkig film

Plural (many boring films):

Hint: Use "många" or "två"
Answer: Många tråkiga filmer (Many boring films)
Exercise 3: Complex Adjectives Advanced
Fill in all forms of the adjective:

Min _____ (ny) bil är _____ (ny). Båda _____ (ny) bilarna är snabba.

Answer: ny / nytt / nya (My new car is new. Both new cars are fast)

Helping Verbs + Infinitive

🎯 Helping Verbs (Hjälpverb)

KAN - can/able to → Han kan simma. (He can swim)

MÅSTE - must/have to → Jag måste jobba. (I must work)

VILL - want → Vi vill äta pizza. (We want to eat pizza)

SKA - will/going to (future) → Hon ska komma imorgon. (She's going to come tomorrow)

Pattern: HELPING VERB + INFINITIVE (base form of other verb)

Exercise 1: KAN vs MÅSTE Beginner
Fill in KAN or MÅSTE:

Du _____ plugga svenska för att klara provet.

Answer: måste (You must study Swedish to pass the test)
Exercise 2: Complete the Infinitive Intermediate
Fill in the helping verb and infinitive:

Jag _____ (vill) _____ (köpa) en ny lägenhet.

Answer: vill / köpa (I want to buy a new apartment)
Exercise 3: SKA for Future Intermediate
Translate using SKA:

"I'm going to study tonight."

Answer: Jag ska plugga ikväll. (I'm going to study tonight)

Possessive Pronouns & Pronouns

🎯 Possessive Pronouns

MIN/MITT/MINA - my | DIN/DITT/DINA - your

HANS - his | HENNES - her | VÅRT/VÅR/VÅRA - our

ER/ERT/ERA - your (plural) | DERAS - their

min bil (my car), mitt hus (my house), mina böcker (my books)

Exercise 1: Mine/Yours/His/Hers Beginner
Fill in the correct possessive pronoun:

Det är _____ (I) lägenhet och _____ (he) lägenhet.

Answer: min / hans (It's my apartment and his apartment)
Exercise 2: Gender Agreement with Possessives Intermediate
Fill in the correct form (min/mitt/mina):

_____ auto, _____ hus, _____ djur

Answer: min bil / mitt hus / mina djur

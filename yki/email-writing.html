Task 1: Informal Email

📧 Scenario
Your Swedish friend Anna has just told you she got a new job in Helsinki. Write an informal email to congratulate her and suggest meeting up soon.
✓ Requirements (50-80 words)
  • Informal greeting (Hej Anna,)
  • React to her news (Vad roligt! / Jag är glad för dig!)
  • Ask how she's feeling or get more details
  • Suggest meeting up (Vi borde träffas!)
  • Informal closing (Kram, / Hälsning,)
📝 Email Structure
Från: [your name] Till: anna@email.com Ärende: Grattis på det nya jobbet! Hej Anna, [Greeting + how are you] [React to news - congratulate] [Ask question or suggest something] [Suggest meeting] [Informal ending] [Your name]
💡 Helpful Phrases
• Vad roligt att du har hittat ett nytt jobb! (How exciting that you found a new job!) • Jag är mycket glad för dig. (I'm very happy for you.) • Vad sägs om lördag på vårt favoritkafe? (What do you say about Saturday at our favorite café?) • Vi ses snart! (See you soon!)

Task 2: Formal Email

📧 Scenario
You're interested in renting an apartment you saw online. Write a formal email to the landlord asking about availability and details about the apartment.
✓ Requirements (60-80 words)
  • Formal greeting (Hej,)
  • Clear reason for writing (Jag skriver angående...)
  • Provide relevant information (dates, apartment number, etc.)
  • Ask specific questions about the apartment
  • Thank them in advance (Tack på förhand)
  • Formal closing (Vänliga hälsningar)
📝 Email Structure
Från: [your name] Till: landlord@email.com Ärende: Förfrågan om lägenhet på Kalastranden 5 Hej, Jag heter [your name] och skriver angående [lägenhet/apartment description]. [Relevant details - when you're looking, your situation] Skulle ni kunna berätta...? [Ask questions about apartment, rent, availability] Tack på förhand. Jag ser fram emot ert svar. Vänliga hälsningar, [Your full name] [Your phone number] [Your email]
💡 Helpful Phrases
• Jag heter X och skriver angående lägenheten... (I am X and I'm writing about the apartment...) • Jag har bott i Finland i 2 år. (I have lived in Finland for 2 years.) • Skulle ni kunna berätta om...? (Could you tell me about...?) • Jag ser fram emot ert svar. (I look forward to your response.) • Vänliga hälsningar (Kind regards)

📚 Email Writing Tips

Informal vs Formal:

  • INFORMAL: "Hej Anna," / "Kram, [name]" / Ask casual questions / Use simple words
  • FORMAL: "Hej," / "Vänliga hälsningar, [full name]" / Be respectful / Use complete sentences

Email Structure Checklist:

  • Header: Från (From), Till (To), Ärende (Subject)
  • Greeting appropriate to tone
  • Opening: Acknowledge/thank/explain why you're writing
  • Body: Main information and questions
  • Closing: Thank you, expectation, or invitation
  • Sign-off: Name and contact info

Common Mistakes to Avoid:

  • ❌ Too short (doesn't meet word count)
  • ❌ Wrong tone (informal when formal is needed)
  • ❌ Missing subject line (Ärende)
  • ❌ Not answering all points of the task
  • ❌ Grammar errors (verb position, adjective agreement)
  • ❌ Too complicated - use simple, clear language

📚 Vocabulary Learning Tips

1. Flashcard Method: Use Quizlet or write words on cards. Swedish on one side, English on the other. Review daily for 15 minutes.

2. Context Learning: Always learn words in sentences, not isolation. The example sentences on cards above show you how each word is used.

3. Spaced Repetition: Review vocabulary you learned:

  • After 1 day
  • After 3 days
  • After 1 week
  • After 2 weeks

4. Speaking Practice: Say words out loud as you study. Pronunciation matters for the YKI exam!

5. Listening: Listen to Swedish songs, podcasts, or YouTube videos. Try to catch vocabulary words you've learned.

6. Theme-Based Study: Focus on one theme at a time. This way you learn related vocabulary together, which helps memory.

# YKI Skriva: Swedish writing in 10 days

A self-contained course for the writing part of the Swedish YKI test (Allmän språkexamen, intermediate level).

## Start

1. Unzip the folder anywhere on your computer or phone.
2. Open `index.html` in a browser (Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari). Double-clicking it is enough.
3. Follow "Today". Each day has a short lesson, a memorising session, a drill set and a writing task. Allow 75–90 minutes.

Nothing is installed and nothing is sent anywhere. Your progress is saved in that browser on that device. To move it to another device, use Library → "Your progress file".

## What is inside

- **Today**: the ten-day plan with your progress.
- **Lessons**: ten short lessons, one skill each, with built-in checks.
- **Memorise**: 103 ready-made sentences and 183 words for the seven themes, on a spaced-repetition schedule. You always type the Swedish from memory.
- **Drills**: 132 quick items on word order, linking words, prepositions, tone, verb forms and endings, plus an error hunt.
- **Write**: 24 exam-style tasks (informal message, formal message, opinion piece) across the seven themes, a timer, an answer sheet, automatic checks, a self-rating scale based on the official criteria, model answers with English, and two full mock exams.
- **Library**: printable sheets: the three text frames, the phrase bank, grammar for writing (extracted from the class notes), the word lists, and the exam-day guide.

## About the checks on your texts

The automatic checks look for patterns: missing task points, greeting and ending, linking words, and the classic word-order and small-word mistakes. They cannot read meaning and they can be wrong. For a real correction, use the "Copy the request" button under any text and paste it into a chat with Claude, or give the text to your teacher.

## The seven themes

The themes follow the chapters of *Förbered dig för allmän språkexamen* (Gimara Oy, 2022): Människan och omgivningen, Vardagsliv, Natur och miljö, Hälsa och välmående, Fritid och hobbyer, Arbete och utbildning, Samhälle. The book itself is not included or reproduced. All tasks, model answers, word lists and exercises here were written for this course. If you own the book, its "Att skriva" pages (38, 54, 64, 75, 90, 116, 125) give you more tasks to practise with the same frames.

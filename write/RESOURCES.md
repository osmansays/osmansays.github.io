# Swedish YKI writing: resources

## Knowledge

- [Official sample tasks: Swedish, intermediate level, writing (University of Jyväskylä)](https://ykitesti.solki.jyu.fi/en/tutustu-testiin/svenska/mellanniva-skriftlig-framstallning/)
  The three task types exactly as the test makers present them: a message to a friend, an opinion piece with a choice of two topics, a written complaint. Use for: what a task looks like, what the bullets demand. The primary source for the whole course.
- [Kriterier för bedömning av skrift (University of Jyväskylä, PDF, in Swedish)](https://www.jyu.fi/fi/file-download/download/public/14102)
  The rating criteria for writing, level by level. Use for: what separates level 2, 3 and 4; the self-rating rubric in the app is adapted from it.
- [Beskrivning av färdighetsnivåerna (Finnish National Agency for Education, PDF)](https://www.oph.fi/sites/default/files/documents/beskrivning-av-fardighetsnivaerna.pdf)
  One-paragraph description of each level per skill. Use for: the plain statement of what level 3 writing is.
- [Proving your language skills with the YKI test (Finnish Immigration Service)](https://migri.fi/en/national-certificate-of-language-proficiency-yki-)
  Which subtest pairs count for citizenship. Use for: why writing matters and what else is needed.
- [Before the YKI test (Finnish National Agency for Education)](https://www.oph.fi/en/education-and-qualifications/yki-test)
  Test-day practicalities: ID, pencil provided, no devices, about five hours. Use for: lesson 10 and the exam-day sheet.
- [Yle: Ruotsin kielen YKI-treenit, keskitaso](https://yle.fi/a/20-146812)
  Free grammar and vocabulary exercises aimed at YKI levels 3–4 (instructions in Finnish). Use for: extra structure practice after the drills here.
- [English sample tasks, intermediate writing (University of Jyväskylä)](https://ykitesti.solki.jyu.fi/en/tutustu-testiin/in-english/intermediate-level-writing/)
  The same three task types in English. Use for: understanding the task format without a language barrier.
- Class notes: "Grupp Rosenkära / YKI autumn 2026" (the learner's own, 23 June – 21 July 2026)
  A1–A2 grammar and vocabulary. Use for: the foundations each lesson builds on. The writing-relevant parts are extracted in `reference/grammar.html`.
- Book: *Förbered dig för allmän språkexamen* (Gimara Oy, 2022), the learner's own copy
  Speaking and writing exercises in seven themes. Use for: more writing tasks per theme ("Att skriva", pages 38, 54, 64, 75, 90, 116, 125). The book is copyrighted and is not reproduced in this workspace; the tasks here are original and follow the same themes and task types.

## Wisdom (Communities)

- The learner's own class, Grupp Rosenkära, and its teacher
  Use for: a human correction of written texts. This is the highest-value feedback available; the book itself recommends asking a teacher or a friend who knows Swedish well.
- [Finland Forum: "Did you pass YKI kirjoittaminen? If yes, share experience"](https://www.finlandforum.org/viewtopic.php?p=575048)
  First-hand reports from candidates on the writing subtest (mostly the Finnish-language test; the format is the same). Use for: what the room, the timing and the tasks felt like.
- Local library language cafés (språkkafé)
  Use for: showing a text to a Swedish speaker and hearing how they would say it.

## Gaps

- No official source states the length of the intermediate writing subtest. Preparation providers give 54–55 minutes for three tasks. The course trains on 54 and tells the learner to check the invitation letter.
- No official word-count targets exist. The ranges used here (60–90, 90–120, 120–160) are working targets.
- The official sample page shows one example per task type and no rated sample answers in Swedish. Rated examples at levels 2, 3 and 4 would sharpen the self-rating rubric.

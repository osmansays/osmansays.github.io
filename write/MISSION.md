# Mission: Swedish YKI writing

## Why
Pass the writing subtest of the Swedish YKI test (Allmän språkexamen, intermediate level) in Finland at level 3 or better, with ten days to prepare. Level 3 in writing, paired with speaking or listening, is what the Finnish Immigration Service accepts as proof of language skills for citizenship.

## Success looks like
- Write a complete informal message (60–90 words) in about 12 minutes that answers every bullet of the task.
- Write a formal message or complaint (90–120 words) in about 17 minutes with the right greeting, tone and sign-off.
- Write an opinion piece (120–160 words) in about 22 minutes with a stated view, three reasons and a conclusion.
- Finish all three inside 54 minutes, by hand, and check them with the three-pass routine.
- Produce the 103 core sentences and most of the 183 theme words from memory.

## Constraints
- Ten days, roughly 75–90 minutes a day.
- Starting point: A1–A2 from the class "Grupp Rosenkära / YKI autumn 2026" (notes up to 21 July 2026).
- Explanations in English; all practice in Swedish as used in Finland.
- Practice topics follow the seven themes of the preparation book *Förbered dig för allmän språkexamen* (Gimara, 2022).
- The course must work offline as the only resource.

## Out of scope
- The speaking, listening and reading subtests.
- Grammar beyond what the three writing tasks need.
- Level 5–6 writing.

## Assumptions to confirm with the learner
- Target is the intermediate test, aiming at level 3 (level 4 if it comes).
- The exam date is about ten days after starting. Set the real date under Library → Settings.

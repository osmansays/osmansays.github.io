# Starting point: A1–A2 from the class notes

The learner's class notes (Grupp Rosenkära, 23 June – 21 July 2026) cover: pronunciation, present tense, verb-second word order with the fundament table, `inte` in main clauses, helper verbs + infinitive, the past tense (four verb groups and common irregulars), `som`, noun gender, definite forms and plurals, adjective agreement, common prepositions and time expressions, and the linkers `och, så, men, eller, dessutom, därför, eftersom`. This is disclosed prior knowledge at exposure depth, not yet shown in the learner's own writing.

## Implications
- Lessons 2 and 7 revise rather than introduce verb-second order and noun/adjective endings.
- Three things the exam needs are absent from the notes and are taught as new: the perfect tense (lesson 6), word order in sub-clauses (lesson 3), and formal register with ni/er (lessons 4 and 7).
- The notes contain no text frames for messages, complaints or opinion pieces; lessons 1, 4 and 5 supply them.
